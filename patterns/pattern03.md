## Pattern 03: Dark Variation

Pattern 03 pulls back from the regular groove. Each 16-row section has two kicks followed by a late snare, while the pad descends chromatically:

```text
D#4 -> D-4 -> C#4 -> C-4
```

Channel 4 alternates between bells and stabs using tritone relationships. This gives the section a colder, less settled character without introducing a conventional melody.

Pattern 03 occurs twice:

```text
Pattern 01 -> Pattern 03 -> Pattern 04
Pattern 05 -> Pattern 03 -> Pattern 06
```

Its ending is therefore deliberately unresolved enough to feed either a build or a breakdown.

Everything not shown should remain empty.

The additional effect is:

```text
C18 = set channel volume to hexadecimal 18
    = decimal 24
```

That keeps the two `Stabs` events quieter than their normal sample volume. The `ExBells` remain at the already-tested `C20`.

The intended shape is:

```text
Rows 00-15:
D# pad, C pedal, then an F# bass tension note
Bell at G-4

Rows 16-31:
Pad slips down to D
Bass moves through G# and D#
Stab answers at C#5

Rows 32-47:
Pad descends to C#
Bass touches C and C#
Bell answers at F#4

Rows 48-63:
Pad reaches C
Bass moves D# -> A# -> G
Final C-5 stab leaves the phrase unresolved
```
