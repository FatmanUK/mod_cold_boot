Pattern roles:
00 - Wake Pulse: Sparse intro. Kick, low bass hints, cold pad fades in.
01 - Main Thump A: Core groove. Thumping kick, bass pulse, restrained hat.
02 - Main Thump B: Same machinery, slightly altered bass movement and more tension.
03 - Dark Variation: Pad harmony shifts. Bell appears briefly. More suspense than melody.
04 - Pressure Build: Denser hats, stab accents, rising energy.
05 - Diagnostic Chime: The “computer is thinking dangerous thoughts” section. Bell motif over pulse.
06 - Breakdown: Drums thin out. Bass and pad carry the unease.
07 - Climax Pulse: Fullest version: kick, bass, hat, stabs/chimes.
08 - Loop Return: Dissolves back toward Pattern 00 cleanly.
