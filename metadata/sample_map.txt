Yes. For Cold Boot, only the sustained tonal samples should be looped.

Use forward loops only. ProTracker MODs use simple forward looping, not ping-pong or fancy sustain modes. So in MilkyTracker, keep it old-school and set loop start plus loop end on the same straight segment.

Recommended loop plan:

01 BassDrum1: no loop
02 Snare4: no loop
03 CloseHiHat: no loop
04 PopBass: loop it, forward loop
05 KorgString: loop it, forward loop
06 ExBells: no loop
07 Stabs: no loop
08 Sweep: no loop

For the two looped samples:

PopBass
Loop the cleanest steady part of the waveform after the initial attack, ideally on a full cycle or a small integer number of cycles. If the sample is already a neat single-cycle bass, loop the whole stable region. If the beginning has a click or transient, start the loop after that. Keep the loop short and seamless.

KorgString
Loop a stable sustain region after the attack. This is the one most likely to benefit from a slightly longer loop than the bass. Again, aim for a smooth, repeatable section with no obvious wobble at the seam.

A simple rule of thumb:

bass loop: very short, tight, cycle-consistent
pad loop: short to medium, smooth sustain
everything else: one-shot

If any ST-01 sample already arrives with loop markers that sound smooth, you can keep them. If not, just set them manually in MilkyTracker by zooming in and finding a clean zero-crossing area with minimal discontinuity. Tiny seam, big magic.
